1. Layout managers

Component	        Layout manager	                    Reason
Main Frame	        BorderLayout	        Divides the window into regions
Search panel	    FlowLayout	            Arranges search controls in a row
Bottom panel	    FlowLayout	            Arranges navigation buttons horizontally
South panel	        BorderLayout	        Separates navigation buttons and status
Results area	    BorderLayout.CENTER	    Expands when the window is resized

2. Nested container structure

Frame – BorderLayout
│
├── North – Title Label
├── Search Panel – FlowLayout
│   ├── Search Label
│   ├── TextField
│   ├── Choice
│   └── Search Button
│
├── Center – TextArea
│
└── South Panel – BorderLayout
    ├── Center – Button Panel (FlowLayout)
    │   ├── Prev
    │   ├── Next
    │   └── Reserve
    └── South – Status Label


3. Why are layout managers important?
Layout managers automatically position and resize components. They help maintain a consistent
interface across different screen sizes without manually specifying component coordinates.
